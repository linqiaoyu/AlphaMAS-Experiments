# AAPL Case Analysis

## OBSERVED

M2, A1 and A2 each had 0.00% cumulative return, 0.00% exposure, zero turnover, zero fills and zero transaction cost. Their final actions differed (M2 0/22/4, A1 0/20/6, A2 0/21/5 BUY/HOLD/SELL), but none issued a PM BUY while in cash. M2 and A2 each processed 23 applied online updates for AAPL; A1 archived 25 matured credits and performed zero parameter updates. M1, by contrast, participated at 45.56% exposure and returned 29.09%.

## INTERPRETATION

For AAPL, parameter evolution and RL-level action changes did not cross the economic threshold required for an executed entry. This demonstrates that learning-state mutation and even pathway overrides are not equivalent to portfolio participation.

## UNRESOLVED

The frozen records establish the pathway but do not identify a single causal reason why PM never authorised a BUY. Post-hoc policy redesign on this evaluation window would violate the no-M3 rule.
