The Aggressive Analyst is treating the absence of selling as a bullish indicator, but that’s a logical error. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence. The Conservative Analyst is manufacturing a bearish signal where none exists.

The trend is intact, price is above all key moving averages, and the fundamentals are solid. Selling into strength without evidence of a reversal is just as risky as buying into weakness without evidence of a bounce.

But that doesn’t mean the trend will continue indefinitely. It means we’re in a period of uncertainty. The right response to uncertainty is not to swing for the fences or to hide in the bunker. It’s to maintain a position that lets us participate in the upside while protecting against the downside. That’s exactly what a HOLD with a wider stop and a disciplined add-on-pullback plan does. The Aggressive Analyst is too eager to add, and the Conservative Analyst is too eager to reduce. The balanced approach is to do neither until the market gives us a clearer signal—either a breakout above $190.53 on strong volume, or a pullback to the $182-186 zone on strong volume. Until then, we hold, we watch, and we don’t let either fear or greed make our decisions for us.
