Trimming before the resistance test means you are selling into a rising trend on the assumption of failure, not on evidence of failure. That is not risk management; that is capitulation to fear.

But the rally lacks volume confirmation, the stock is approaching a resistance zone it has not tested since late March, and the 20-day return is still negative. Do not add at $184.66—the risk/reward to the resistance zone is not compelling enough to justify increasing exposure. Do not trim either—the trend is up, the momentum is fresh, and selling now would lock in gains prematurely. Instead, hold the benchmark-weight position. If the stock breaks above $190.06 on volume, that is the confirmation to add. If it fails at $188.88–$190.06 and falls back below the 50-day SMA at $180.59, that is the signal to reduce.
