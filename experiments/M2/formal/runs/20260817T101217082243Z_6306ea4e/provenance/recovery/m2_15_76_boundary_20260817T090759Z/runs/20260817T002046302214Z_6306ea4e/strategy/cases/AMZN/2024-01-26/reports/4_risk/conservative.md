We are being asked to deploy capital into a position where we cannot identify a single catalyst, a single piece of news, or a single macro indicator to support the move. The Aggressive Analyst says “no news is good news.” I say no news means we are flying blind. In the absence of a catalyst, the stock is trading purely on technical momentum, and technical momentum can reverse in an instant.

Missing a trade is not a loss. It is an opportunity cost. But buying at $159.12 with a stop at $155.50 and no fundamental support is a guaranteed way to lose money. The firm’s objective is to protect assets and ensure steady growth. That means we do not chase extended moves into resistance with incomplete data. We wait for a better entry, or we do not enter at all.
