But it also means we have no income statement data, no revenue growth, no margins. We are flying blind on the most important fundamental metrics. The aggressive analyst is extrapolating from a balance sheet to justify a momentum trade. The conservative analyst is using the absence of data to justify inaction. Both are wrong. The correct response to missing data is to reduce position size, not to either go all-in or stay out entirely.
