But the fundamental quality and oversold conditions argue against a full exit. The balanced, risk-aware stance is to trim exposure and wait for confirmation — either a reclaim of the 10 EMA ($172.85) or a MACD bullish crossover — before re-engaging. This respects the bear's valid technical warning while acknowledging the bull's legitimate point that this is a quality asset at a discount, not a broken business.
