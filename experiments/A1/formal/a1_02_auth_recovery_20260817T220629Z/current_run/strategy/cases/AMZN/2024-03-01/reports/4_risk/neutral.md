That is not a "moving target that has already been tested multiple times" as a weakness; that is a level that has proven its reliability. Yes, it is a small positive number, but it represents a directional change from negative to positive. That is the definition of a fresh bullish crossover. Dismissing it because the magnitude is small is like saying a green traffic light is not really green because it is not bright enough.

This allows us to participate in continued upside if the trend persists, while limiting downside to a defined level. It is not a BUY because we do not have sufficient evidence to justify adding exposure at a 60-day peak with declining volume and no news catalyst. It is not a SELL because the trend is intact and the fundamentals are strong. It is a disciplined HOLD that acknowledges the uncertainty in both directions.

Both are right in their own frameworks, but both are wrong for the actual situation. The actual situation is a strong uptrend with genuine uncertainty about near-term direction.
