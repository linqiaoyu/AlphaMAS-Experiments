That sounds prudent, but it ignores the reality that the stock is at $163.46, sitting right in the middle of that range. Waiting for either extreme means you’re either buying after a 4% drop or chasing after a 2% breakout. Neither is a superior risk/reward to simply holding what you already own.

The Aggressive Analyst is wrong because a fortress balance sheet from Q3 2023 tells us nothing about Q4 net interest income, credit provisions, or trading revenue. The Conservative Analyst is wrong because the absence of data is not the same as the presence of risk. We have no negative sentiment, no negative news, and no negative macro signal.
