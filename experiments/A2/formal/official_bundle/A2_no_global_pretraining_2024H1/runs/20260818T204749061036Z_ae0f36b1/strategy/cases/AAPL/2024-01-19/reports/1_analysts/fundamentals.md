3. **Massive Asset Base:** $352.6 billion in assets provides substantial financial flexibility and collateral capacity.

### Risks / Considerations
1. **High Leverage:** With liabilities at 82.4% of assets, Apple relies heavily on debt financing. However, this is largely a deliberate capital structure choice to optimize the cost of capital while returning cash to shareholders.
2. **Thin Equity Cushion:** $62.1 billion equity against $352.6 billion assets means limited downside absorption capacity in a severe stress scenario, though Apple's cash flows mitigate this concern.
3. **Heavy Financing Outflows:** The -$108.5 billion financing outflow indicates ongoing reliance on debt markets to fund buybacks/dividends. If credit conditions tighten, this could pressure the capital return program.
