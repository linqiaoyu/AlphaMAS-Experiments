The fundamental strength provides a floor that argues against aggressive shorting or outright exit for long-term holders.

The balanced conclusion: the technical trend is clearly down and momentum is deteriorating, which argues for reducing exposure rather than adding. But the fundamental quality and proximity to support argue against a full exit. This is a trim, not a liquidation — an Underweight stance that respects the bear's superior technical discipline while acknowledging the bull's legitimate fundamental points.
