It tells us the company can fund its own growth, which reduces downside risk. Her demand for “news validation” is also problematic—we’ve established that news is unavailable in this historical window. Demanding data that doesn’t exist is not prudence; it’s paralysis.

The conservative analyst’s “wait for $160–$167” is too restrictive—that’s a 7.9% drop that may not come.

Half is the balanced answer.
